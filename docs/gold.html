How It Works

Gold Drafting System

Devised by Adam Gold (2012 Sloan) via Micah Blake McCurdy. When a team is mathematically eliminated from playoffs, their Gold clock starts. Every win after elimination earns 2 Gold Points. Most Gold Points = #1 draft pick. No lottery — compete to draft high.

Magic Numbers
  • Win Magic Number — smallest number of wins guaranteeing a playoff spot even if every other conference team wins all their remaining games.
  • Lose Elimination Number — losses until it becomes mathematically impossible to make the playoffs, even winning every remaining game.
Elimination Algorithm

Based on Appendix B of Adam Gold (2010), adapted for 8 playoff spots per conference. For each team, the algorithm finds the 8 teams that can best block them — teams that can each accumulate more points than the tested team's maximum, even while losing to the tested team. Both the wildcard path and division winner path must be blocked for a team to be mathematically eliminated. This is more precise than the simple max-points formula because it accounts for teams playing each other.
