How It Works

Gold Drafting System

Devised by Adam Gold (2012 Sloan Sports Analytics Conference) and popularized by Micah Blake McCurdy (HockeyViz). The traditional draft lottery incentivizes tanking — losing on purpose to get a better pick. Gold fixes this: only wins after elimination earn draft value.

  • When a team is mathematically eliminated from the conference playoff race, their Gold clock starts
  • Every win after that earns 2 Gold Points
  • Draft order = most Gold Points picks first
  • Ties in Gold Points broken by fewer regular-season wins (weaker team picks higher)
  • Playoff teams pick after all eliminated teams
Magic Numbers
  • Win Magic Number — exact number of wins to guarantee a playoff spot, even if all other teams win out. Uses the maximum-points formula: exact in a 2-outcome league.
  • Lose Elimination Number — number of losses until you cannot reach the 8th seed in your conference even winning every remaining game.
Simulation

50,000 Monte Carlo simulations of the real remaining schedule. Each game: P(home wins) = logistic((home_rating − away_rating + HCA) / 11.0). Ratings from ridge regression on all regular-season results. Seeding: 3 division winners per conference (seeds by record among winners), then 5 wildcard spots by best remaining conference record.
